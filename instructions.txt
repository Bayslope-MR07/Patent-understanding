The patent relates to the field of automobiles, specifically to automobile diagnostic methods and systems. Typically, automobile diagnostic systems are used to diagnose problems, faults, and performance of a vehicle. The vehicle connects to an external diagnostic device, such as a tablet or computer, through an interface known as a diagnostic connection device (or vehicle interface). Traditionally, this diagnostic connection device is responsible for collecting data from different vehicle modules (such as engine or brake systems), performing protocol conversions as per standard transmission requirements, and then forwarding the converted data to the diagnostic device. Since the diagnostic connection device is a small hardware unit, this creates a burden on it despite the fact that the external diagnostic device usually has more computing resources available.

The invention solves this problem by reducing the burden on the diagnostic connection device so that the hardware costs of the connection device can be minimized, without adding costs to the diagnostic device side. The patent achieves this by shifting the protocol conversion processes to the diagnostic device. The disclosed system consists of three main parts: an automobile network (the in-vehicle network connecting different modules and units), a diagnostic connection device (acting as a bridge between the vehicle and the external diagnostic system), and the automobile diagnostic device (the remote device that runs diagnostic software). In operation, the automobile diagnostic device sends diagnostic instructions to the automobile network via the diagnostic connection device. The automobile network communicates with the relevant vehicle modules to obtain detection data. This detection data is converted into standard transmission data and then sent to the diagnostic device through the diagnostic connection device. The diagnostic device then parses the received detection data to generate diagnostic results, which are presented to the user. Importantly, the diagnostic device can transmit the standard transmission data synchronously or asynchronously, improving efficiency and flexibility in handling large or multiple packets of data.
